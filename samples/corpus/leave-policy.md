---
title: Annual Leave Policy
groups: all-employees
url: https://intranet.example/docs/leave-policy
---

Employees receive 14 days of annual leave in each of their first five years of service. After five years of service the annual leave entitlement rises to 20 days.

Leave requests are entered in the HR portal at least one week in advance and approved by the line manager. Unused leave carries over for one year.
